- 🤖 Currently working with international teams on AI tool adoption, MCP integrations, and a proprietary software development harness.

- 📚 Interested in electronics, books, programming, and technology with social impact.

## What I work on

At Avanade, I help teams apply AI to their development processes by understanding business requirements, adapting tools to different teams and IDEs, training users, and gathering feedback to guide improvements.

My contributions include proposing layered context for AI agents to improve consistency between backend and frontend deliverables, and building Python telemetry to understand agent usage.

My full-stack background includes **Java, Spring Boot, Angular, TypeScript, REST/GraphQL APIs, and relational databases**, with experience in integrations, authentication, testing, and query optimization.
